Definition

  1. A pair, a couple; originally used of dogs, and later of animals generally (e.g., a brace of conies) and then other things, but rarely human persons. (In British use (as plural), this is a particularly common reference to game birds.)
  2. Humorous coinage from Wondermark #566, “Supernatural Collective Nouns” (Elven class).
