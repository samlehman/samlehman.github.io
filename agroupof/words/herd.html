Definition

  1. A number of domestic animals assembled together under the watch or ownership of a keeper.
  2. Listed in the Book of Saint Albans (1486) as “an Herde of corlewys”.
  3. Listed in the Book of Saint Albans (1486) as “an Herde of harlottys”.
  4. Listed in the Book of Saint Albans (1486) as “AN Herde of hartys”.
  5. Listed in the Book of Saint Albans (1486) as “an Herde of swannnys”.
  6. Listed in the Book of Saint Albans (1486) as “an Herde of wrennys”.
