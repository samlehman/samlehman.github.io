Definition

  1. Listed in the Book of Saint Albans (1486) as “a Rowte of knyghtes”.
  2. A group or assortment of snails.
  3. A group of animals, especially one which is lively or unruly, or made up of wild animals such as wolves; a flock, a herd, a pack.
