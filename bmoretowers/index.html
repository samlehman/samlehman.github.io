Where was this photo taken?

The two broadcast towers on Baltimore's TV Hill can be seen from almost anywhere in the city. Mark their two tips in a photo, and the map shows where the camera could have been.

Choose a photo to start.

How to mark the tips
  1. Candelabra (red lattice tower with three masts on a triangular crown): click the top of its tallest mast.
  2. WBFF (thin grey guyed mast, the taller of the two): click its very tip.
  3. Zoom in to place them precisely: scroll or pinch, drag to pan, or drag a marker to adjust it. Arrow keys nudge the selected tip by one pixel.
Settings

The map will show the possible camera positions once both tips are marked.
