Trolls

A supernatural creature (either a dwarf or a giant) that is supposed to live in caves or in the mountains (Scandanavian folklore). WordNet

What is a group of trolls called?

There is no established collective noun for trolls, but a group of trolls is jokingly called a malevolence.
